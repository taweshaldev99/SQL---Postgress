# 72-HOUR EMERGENCY CASH PLAN

**Assets:** laptop, wifi, time, $0.
**Deadline:** rent due in 3 days.
**Rule:** two tracks run in parallel — (A) buy time on the rent, (B) generate same-day cash.
Track A is worth more per hour spent than anything else on this list. Do it first.

> Region note: items marked **[US]** are US-specific. Equivalents for other regions are noted inline.

---

## HOUR 0–2 — BUY TIME (highest value per minute)

### 1. Message your landlord. Today. Before you have the money. (20 min)
Counterintuitive but true: eviction costs a landlord 1–3 months of lost rent plus filing fees.
A tenant who communicates early with a dated plan is a *far* better outcome for them than one who
goes silent. Silence is what triggers filings. Do NOT wait until you've missed the date.

Copy-paste, edit the numbers:

> Hi [Name] — I want to give you a heads up directly rather than have you find out when the payment
> doesn't land. I lost my job on [date]. I can pay [$X] on [date within 3 days] and the remaining
> [$Y] by [specific date, 2–4 weeks out]. I've already filed for unemployment and I have work lined
> up. I've never been late before [if true]. Can we put this in writing so we're both covered?
> I'd rather solve this with you than have it become a bigger problem for either of us.

Rules: name a **specific** dollar amount and **specific** dates. Vague promises get rejected.
Any partial payment dramatically improves your position. Get the agreement in writing (text is fine).

### 2. File for unemployment TODAY (30 min) **[US / UK / EU / most of Canada, AU]**
- Benefits typically **backdate to your termination date**, but only if you file — every day you
  delay can be a day you lose. Do not self-disqualify by assuming you're ineligible.
- **[US]** Your state UI website. Also check if your state has expedited/emergency processing.
- **[UK]** Universal Credit — ask for an **Advance Payment**, paid within days.
- **[India/Nepal]** No direct UI equivalent — skip to step 3 and 4.

### 3. Money you are already owed (30 min)
- **Final paycheck.** In many US states an employer must pay final wages within days of
  termination — some require it immediately on involuntary termination. Email HR/payroll today
  asking for the date and amount.
- **Accrued PTO/vacation payout** — owed in many jurisdictions. Ask in the same email.
- **Deposits:** old utility deposits, gym/subscription refunds, security deposit on a prior place.
- **Tax refund status** — check if one is pending or unfiled.

### 4. Emergency rental assistance (45 min) **[US]**
These organizations frequently pay the landlord **directly**, sometimes within 24–72 hours:
- Dial **211** (or 211.org) — the single highest-yield call. Ask specifically for
  "emergency rental assistance" and "eviction prevention."
- Salvation Army, St. Vincent de Paul, Catholic Charities — do not need to be a member or religious.
- Your local church / mosque / temple / gurdwara benevolence fund — these often cut same-week checks
  with minimal paperwork.
- Local Community Action Agency (search "[your county] community action agency").
- **[UK]** Council Discretionary Housing Payment + local welfare assistance scheme.

**Expected value of Hour 0–2: potentially the entire rent. Do not skip it to go "hustle."**

## HOUR 2–8 — FASTEST REAL CASH (highest certainty)

Ranked by certainty, not by how interesting they are.

### 1. Sell physical items — cash in hand today (~$100–600, very high certainty)
This is the most reliable money in this document. Go through your home now and list everything you
own that is worth >$40 and that you can live without for six months.

Typical: old phone, tablet, game console + games, bike, power tools, camera, headphones, monitor,
guitar, watch, jewelry, designer clothing, textbooks, kitchen appliances.

- **Facebook Marketplace**, local pickup, cash only. Fastest liquidity by far.
- Price at **~60% of what comparable listings ask** and write "PRICE FIRM, TODAY ONLY, CASH."
  You are optimizing for speed, not for maximum price. This is the whole trick.
- List all items within the same 30 minutes. Respond to messages within 2 minutes.
- **[US]** Also: OfferUp, Craigslist. **[India/Nepal]** OLX, Facebook Marketplace.
- Same-day electronics buyback: **[US]** ecoATM kiosks (instant cash for phones, lowball but instant).

### 2. Pawn — only if step 1 falls short (instant, but costly)
You get roughly 25–50% of resale value and pay heavy interest to redeem. Use for items you
genuinely intend to buy back. Do this *after* trying a direct sale.

### 3. Plasma donation (~$100–150 in your first week) **[US]**
- First-time donor bonuses are large; you can donate twice in 7 days. Paid to a debit card same day.
- CSL Plasma, BioLife, Octapharma, Grifols. Bring ID, SSN, proof of address. ~90 min first visit.
- Not available as paid donation in most of Europe / UK / India / Nepal — skip if so.

### 4. Same-day-pay gig work
The key feature is **instant payout**, not the hourly rate:
- **DoorDash** (Fast Pay), **Uber Eats** (Instant Pay), **Instacart** (Instant Cashout),
  **Lyft** (Express Pay). Bike or scooter is enough in dense cities.
- **[US]** Amazon Flex, Wonolo, Instawork, Bluecrew — same/next-day shift work, no vehicle needed
  for warehouse/event shifts.
- **TaskRabbit** — furniture assembly, moving help, hauling. Pays out fast, no vehicle needed for many.
- Craigslist "gigs" section — day labor, moving help, event setup. Cash same day. Meet in public,
  never pay a fee to get work.
- **[India/Nepal]** Swiggy/Zomato/Zepto delivery, Urban Company, local daily-wage boards.

## HOUR 8–72 — WHERE AI ACTUALLY MAKES YOU MONEY

Read this carefully because it's where most people waste their 72 hours.

**What does NOT work in 72 hours** (do not spend a single hour on these):
- Building an app, SaaS, or "AI tool." Zero chance of revenue by Friday.
- Upwork / Fiverr for *this* deadline — funds clear in 7–14 days. Set them up for week 2, not now.
- Selling AI-generated ebooks / prompt packs / "faceless" content. Saturated, near-zero conversion,
  and it burns hours you cannot spare.
- Anything that asks you to pay a fee first. Anything.

**What DOES work:** selling a *concrete, finished deliverable* to a *local small business* for
*payment on delivery* via Zelle/Venmo/PayPal/UPI/cash. AI compresses a 6-hour job into 30 minutes —
so your bottleneck is outreach volume, not production. **Send 50 messages, not 5.**

### Play 1: Your existing network (do this FIRST — 10–20x the conversion of cold outreach)
Message 40 people individually — ex-colleagues, ex-managers, former clients, friends who run
businesses. Not a public post. Individual messages.

> Hey [Name] — I got laid off from [company] last week and I'm picking up freelance work while I
> job hunt. I'm doing [specific thing: data cleanup / reports / websites / spreadsheets / content].
> If you or anyone you know needs something like that — even something small this week — I'd really
> appreciate the intro. I can turn things around in 24 hours.

Ask for the intro explicitly. Most people want to help and just need to be told how.

### Play 2: Local business cold outreach — the $150 same-day offer
Target: restaurants, dentists, gyms, salons, contractors, real estate agents, auto shops, clinics.
Find them on Google Maps. Find ones with bad/no website, no recent posts, unanswered reviews.

Offer a **fixed price, finished today, paid on delivery**. Specificity closes; "I do marketing" does not.

Offers you can genuinely deliver with AI in under an hour each:
1. **Google Business Profile overhaul** — rewrite description, categories, services, 20 photos
   captioned, plus written replies to every unanswered review. **$150.**
2. **30 days of social posts** — captions + hashtags + posting schedule, delivered as a spreadsheet. **$200.**
3. **One-page website** — HTML/CSS, their info, mobile-friendly, deployed free on Netlify/Vercel. **$250–400.**
4. **Menu / price list / flyer redesign** — clean, print-ready. **$100.**
5. **Email templates pack** — booking confirmations, follow-ups, review requests, no-show recovery. **$150.**
6. **Spreadsheet / data cleanup** — dedupe their customer list, fix formatting, build a summary
   dashboard. **$150–300.** ← *You've been learning SQL. This is directly in your lane and most
   small businesses have a genuinely awful customer spreadsheet.*

Cold email/DM script:

> Subject: your Google listing — free sample attached
>
> Hi [Name], I'm local and I noticed [SPECIFIC observation: "you have 43 reviews and 31 have no
> reply" / "your hours are wrong on Google" / "your menu PDF doesn't open on phones"].
>
> I've attached [the first 5 review replies I wrote for you / a redesigned version of your menu] so
> you can see the quality — that part's yours to keep either way.
>
> I'll finish the whole thing today for $150. You only pay when you see it and like it.
>
> [Your name] — [phone]

Why this works: you did the work *before* asking for money, it's free to say yes, and there's no risk
to them. **Attach real work.** That single move is the difference between 0% and ~5–10% response.

Math: 50 messages → ~5 replies → 1–2 sales → $150–400. Send 100 and it roughly doubles.
Walking in physically to 15 businesses with a laptop beats 50 emails — do both.

### Play 3: AI training / data work — apply now, pays week 2
Approval takes 2–7 days so it won't hit Friday, but **apply in the next hour** so money lands next week:
- **Mercor**, **Outlier / Scale AI**, **Surge AI**, **Handshake AI** — pay $20–50/hr for people with
  coding/technical backgrounds. Your SQL work qualifies you for data/SQL annotation tracks.
- **Prolific** — research studies, smaller amounts, but comparatively fast payout.
- **Clickworker**, **Appen** — lower rate, easier entry.

## REALISTIC OUTCOME

| Source | Range | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| Selling possessions | $100–600 | High |
| Rental assistance / landlord deferral | $0 – full rent | Medium, huge upside |
| Plasma **[US]** | $100–150 | High |
| Gig apps (2 days) | $80–300 | Medium (vehicle-dependent) |
| Local AI services | $0–800 | Low-medium, highest ceiling |
| Final paycheck / PTO owed | Varies | Medium |

Plausible 72-hour total: **$300–900 cash**, plus a real chance the landlord conversation removes the
deadline entirely. That combination is what actually gets you through the week.

---

## DO NOT

- Payday loans / cash-advance apps with fees — 300–600% effective APR, turns a 3-day crisis into a
  9-month one.
- Any "make money with AI" course, community, or coaching. They are selling to people in your exact
  situation. That's the business model.
- Crypto, sports betting, or trading to "make it back fast."
- Any gig requiring you to pay upfront, buy equipment, or "verify" with a deposit.
- Selling bulk AI-generated content on marketplaces — account bans, no revenue.
- Going silent with your landlord. This is the single most expensive mistake available to you.

## AFTER THE 72 HOURS

Rent is the emergency; income is the actual problem. Once Friday is handled:
- Finish the SQL work — data/analyst roles are a realistic target and your repo shows 20 days of
  consistent effort, which is a genuine signal to an employer.
- Turn every local business you served into a **monthly retainer** ($200–500/mo). Recurring revenue
  from 3 clients ≈ a rent payment, permanently.
- Keep the Upwork/Fiverr profiles you set up on Day 1 — that money arrives in week 2 and beyond.
